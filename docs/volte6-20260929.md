## 0. Summary
| item | result |
|---|---|
| Our 770 answers vs stock | **Byte-identical** (re-checked by disassembly, now pinned by a test). Stock `imsdatadaemon` answers LINK_ADDR (0x23), SERVICE_ENABLE_STATUS (0x34) and SUB_DESTROY_INSTANCE (0x33) with `qmi_csi_send_resp` of an 8-byte struct whose result is zeroed (`str xzr,[sp]`), and REGISTER_APP_STATE (0x2e) with a 16-byte struct: result zeroed, and the optional TLV 0x10 left as uninitialised stack. The wire answer is `02 04 00 00 00 00 00` (result TLV only), which is what we send. Stock sends **nothing unsolicited** before the modem asks. Its only other QMI traffic is a **DMS Get Device Capabilities (0x20)** client, used to set `vendor.ims.modem.multisub.cap` (AP-side bookkeeping, nothing sent to the modem). **The 770 protocol is not the blocker (high confidence).** |
| What 0x33 instance=1 means | `instance` is `QmiImsDcmInstanceId` (libqmi 1.37): **1 = GLOBAL**, 2 = ID1 (sub 0), 3 = ID2 (sub 1). The modem tore down its **global** IMS instance. Stock only logs it and answers success (handler 0x18d4c). |
| What the 29 Sep run really tested | **Only one 770 handshake happened, and it was during verify #1, while the SIM had no ATR** (card error=3, PIN refused). Verify #2 (SIM OK, LTE full service) **reused that daemon**, so the modem never sent a new handshake. The GLOBAL destroy came in between, most likely on the SIM/subscription change. After the SIM was up, **nothing on the AP re-asserted IMS for sub 0**: our IMSS step had run once, before the SIM, and did not write because IMS was "already on". |
| What stock does that we did not | (1) Android's ImsService re-sends "IMS on" after every SIM load: `turnOnIms` → qcril IMSS **0x008F** `ims_service_enabled=1` (+ `volte=1`). **qcril does not compare this with the modem state**, so it is sent even when IMS is already on. (2) The VoLTE user preference is sent to NAS: `qcril_qmi_ims_map_ims_volte_user_pref_to_qmi_nas_voice_domain_pref` (0x90c2ec) maps on → **3 (IMS PS preferred)** and off → 2. `qcril_qmi_nas_set_voice_domain_preference` (0x6b67ec) sends NAS **0x0033 with only TLV 0x23 = vdp** (req struct +0xbc valid/+0xc0 value = IDL req TLV 0x23 u32@192). The read-back is the cached 0x0034 response at +0xbc/+0xc0 = **resp TLV 0x20**. |
| Root cause | **Not proven, medium confidence at best.** The ranked hypotheses are in §2. The data shows the network VoPS bit is 0 (`ims_voice_support`, NAS sys-info TLV 0x29) and the LTE voice domain is CS. We cannot tell offline whether this is our missing re-assert/voice-domain-pref (fixable) or Orange refusing VoPS for this device (not fixable on the AP). The volte6 bundle separates the cases in one attended run. |
| Fix (a6l-imsdcm) | **Re-assert like stock** (`A6L_IMSDCM_KICK=force`, the default in volte6-test.sh and the ROM prop `persist.vendor.a6l.ril.imsdcm_kick=force`): IMSS 0x8F `volte=1 ims_service_enabled=1` is written even when already on, then IMSA is re-bound and indications registered again. This runs when LTE reaches full service and 3 s after any 0x33 (at most 3 times). Variant `toggle` writes 0 then 1. **NAS view:** `A6L_IMSDCM_NAS` lines (vdp, usage, VoPS, LTE voice domain, LTE srv), polled. `A6L_IMSDCM_VDP=set` writes vdp=3 exactly as qcril does, only when it is not already 3. **`A6L_IMSDCM_EVENT destroy instance=N (GLOBAL/ID1/…)`**. **`A6L_IMSDCM_IMSA STATE registered=0/1 src=…`** on every change. |

Order rules:
- ipa4 before radio2.
- load/activate before verify: they refuse while a6l-imsdcm runs.
- **The SIM must be seated before step 6.** The 29 Sep verify #1 ran without an ATR, and that made the only handshake of the day worthless. Verify now restarts the daemon (`FRESH=1`), so rerunning verify after reseating the SIM is valid.

- Writes (IMSS set, NAS vdp) go to the RAM EFS copy only. The script refuses them without `rmtfs -o /tmp/…`. A reboot discards them.

## 8. Risks (honest)
- **No confident root cause.** H3 (the network) would make every AP change irrelevant. It is not testable offline: stock never had VoLTE on Orange (Hisense whitelist = CMCC/CT/CU).
- IMSS force/toggle and NAS vdp are modem writes. They are RAM-only here, but **persistent in the ROM** (intended: stock does the same). `toggle` briefly switches IMS off.
- The NAS TLV ids come from the stock qcril struct offsets and the IDL (get 0x20, set 0x23). They are not confirmed on the modem yet: the step-3 read shows whether 0x20 holds a sane value (0..3).
- The kick at LTE full service also fires after every coverage loss (max 3 per daemon run). The ROM daemon should later reset that budget per SIM load.
- `REATTACH=1` sends the PIN twice in one run (guarded: ≥3 retries).

- **Evidence that the 29 Sep activate was applied live:** after the activate, IMSS read `ims_service_enabled=1 volte=1` (ROW would give 0), the modem fs1 RAM copy changed (`4c6bcae6…` → `9e7d56d1…`), and IMSA reported **`ut=available/wwan`**, i.e. the Orange XCAP/Ut config was running. **The Orange IMS settings were live without a restart.**
- **What is left of H2:** the voice domain pref. The NV value is 3 in both MBNs. It is only wrong if stock Android wrote vdp=2 through qcril (Hisense "VoLTE off" for non-CMCC/CT/CU), and that value would persist in the EFS. The `check` step shows it (`A6L_IMSDCM_NAS get vdp=`) **before** anything is written, and VDP=set fixes it before the first attach. **H2 revised: low (≈15%).** `RESTART=1` stays as the last AP-side variant (H4), because it is the only way to make every MBN item, including the NAS/RRC ones, be read at modem boot.
- RESTART risk: the first SSR on this stack. ipa2-lite has an SSR path (upstream), but it is untested here. The worst case is a crashed modem and a reboot. The EFS is RAM-only, so nothing persists.

### R3. H3: Orange's device policy
- Orange's own support page says VoLTE needs a "mobile compatible". An Orange advisor on the Orange forum states: *"les services VoLTE et appels Wi-Fi d'Orange sont compatibles uniquement avec les mobiles présents dans cette liste"*. The Nothing Phone is a documented case: VoLTE-capable and working on Bouygues/SFR/Free, but **refused by Orange for more than 2 years** until Orange listed it. So the check is on the **network side and per device model** (IMEI TAC, which the MME receives as IMEISV at attach). It is not a SIM option.
- The Orange list (reseaux.orange.fr, "appels wifi" page) has **no Hisense model**. The A6L is a China-only device (stock `ro.hmct.volte.operator=cmcc+ct+cu`), so its TAC is almost certainly not listed.
- What this looks like: the attach/TAU accept carries **IMS VoPS = 0**, NAS shows `lte_voice_domain=3 (CS)`, and the modem IMS stack never asks for the IMS PDN (no 770 `PDP_ACTIVATE`). **This is exactly what 29 Sep showed.** An unlisted device **cannot register VoLTE on Orange**, whatever the AP does. Changing the IMEI is illegal and out of scope.
- **H3 revised: high (≈75%).**
