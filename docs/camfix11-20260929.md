**Conclusion:** CSID → ISPIF → VFE RDI0 → WM0 → SMMU → DDR is proven byte-exact at the full size and rate. The fault is sensor → CSIPHY → CSID RX.

**Real-sensor data does reach DDR, in pieces.**

**Sensor frames always carry b16, b25, b27 and b29, and carry b28 in about 57 %.**
- TG frames carry none of them (the TG enters after the RX).
- camfix11 bit0 replaces the guessing with the CSID's own counters (`STATS_TOTAL/ECC/CRC`) and the captured packet headers.

### 1b. CSIPHY common status 0-10 (0x8b0 + 4n)
- Every row, TG and sensor alike: `01 01 20 20 00 04 04 80 80 40 00`.
- The first stream after boot, and t30 PHY2: `05 01 a0 20 00 14 04 80 82 40 04`.
- **Identical whether the frame then passes (TG) or fails (sensor).** So these latched bits do not discriminate.
- The pairs (s0/s1, s2/s3, s5/s6, s7/s8) look like per-lane state that is the same on all lanes.
- Stock would enable masks `FF FF FB FF 7F FF FF EF FF FF FF` on them (ctrl11-21) and only print them. No public bit names exist.

## 2. Ranked hypotheses

1. **HS reception damages the long packets** (≈35 %). The cause would be settle, a PHY common setting or CSID packet handling. The CSID drops or truncates most lines (per-frame error bits, lane FIFO overflows), so the WM trickles.
   - Both sensors fail. s5k3t1 already has the stock settle, which points to the **shared** differences: COMMON_CTRL7 = 0x02 (a C-PHY combo value) and CORE_CTRL_1 0x9 versus 0xF.
   - Tests: `istock`, `ictl1f`, `ip7skip`, `ip7zero`, `iset14/28/10` and `sstock`.
2. **Packet content differs from what is configured** (≈25 %): a different WC or DT, or PDAF DT 0x36 lines interleaved on VC0 for imx576. The unmapped/mapped error bits every frame fit this.
   - Test: the `idiag` HDR histograms show the real WC/DT. Then `idt36` and `istock` (stock two-CID LUT).
3. **CORE_CTRL_1 low bits 1-2** (≈15 %). Stock sets them, mainline does not. They are probably ECC correction or CRC check. This is cheap and shared by both sensors. Test: `ictl1f`, `sstock`.
4. **The sensor outputs something other than 2880 RAW10 on 4 lanes** (≈10 %). Test: `A6L_SNS_RD` (0x0112/0x0113/0x0114/0x034c-f/0x0820-23) plus the long-header WC.
5. **Lane assignment** (≈5 %). It is unlikely: FS/FE short-packet headers are byte-striped over all 4 lanes and decode every frame. `LASSIGN` is available if it is needed.
6. **Other** (≈10 %): CSID clock, clock-lane timing or a board signal-integrity problem.

**Not built: a CSID crop so that an over-long line cannot overrun.** Gen1 CSID/ISPIF RDI has no crop, and the TG proves that the WM handles the configured geometry. HDR WC tells whether lines are over-long.

## 5. Risks
- bit3 raises the CSID IRQ rate for one capture only, and self-caps at 4000 IRQs.
- `ctrl7` -2/0 change a PHY common register. Only the values are changed, and the CSIPHY reset clears them at the next power-up.
- The sensor modules changed. `a6l_rd` only reads registers. `a6l_regs` is empty by default. The source provenance is verified (srcversion).
