## Camera candidate

The isolated candidate corrects JPEG logical BLOB capacity/footer placement,
reserves footer space and bounds encoder overflow without reallocating gralloc
memory. It also preserves measured AE state on non-statistics frames rather
than reporting SEARCHING three frames out of four. These corrections do not
change tonal mapping, black level or the exposure target.

Opt-in diagnostics retain a finite initial frame window to correlate sensor
control requests/readbacks, RAW statistics, AWB, autofocus and ISP processing.
Readback is not mislabeled as frame-effective exposure. This is intended to
identify the washed-out photos and sustained 4.6–4.7 fps software video path;
neither quality nor frame rate is marked fixed. A matching HAL/core/signed IPA
and IPC worker package was built and verified in isolation.

The Auto policy now submits the first eligible, exact-request rear image
without waiting for another capture. Explicit modes and subsequent policy,
backoff, rate limits, scanout checks and successful ACK requirements remain.
Same-pixel switches preserve the existing drive/ACK requirement. Actual-source,
sanitizer, capture/socket/ACK and recovery checks pass; physical saving is
pending. The PM helper also checks space before creating its replacement
snapshot, retaining current log/free-space budgets. This is a per-call guard,
not a strict quota against concurrent writers.

Bridge prewarming, waveform/rail changes, GPU capture and
modern optical ghosting compensation are not silently included. See the latest
four separately explained options in `eink-investigation-20261002.md`.
