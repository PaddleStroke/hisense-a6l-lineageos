# Independent review of the A6L master checklist — 25 September 2026

The checklist covers most major hardware blocks, but it is not yet a complete acceptance checklist for an installed phone. The largest omissions are dual-SIM support, complete Android sensor behavior, radio lifecycle/coexistence, calibration provenance, and several system/security validation gates. Its Turnip plan is technically incorrect for the Adreno 512.

Scope: reviewed `docs/master-todo-20260925.md`, recent delivery notes, device/product/init/HAL sources, saved stock observations and the saved V67 kernel config. Repository HEAD at the start was `9e7af7b` (25 September). No phone, laptop, WSL build, relay, firmware or running Claude job was operated. This is a separate review so Claude can merge it without a concurrent edit to the master checklist. References to current packaging describe the checked-in snapshot, not Claude's unfinished V75 output. The `claude/...` notes cited by the master checklist were not independently available here.

## Corrections to existing entries

1. **H3 — replace the Turnip task.** Adreno 512 is a5xx. Mesa explicitly says Turnip has no plans for a5xx or earlier. Cross-compiling Turnip and setting a property will not provide hardware Vulkan. Keep hardware GLES through freedreno; separately decide whether software Vulkan is useful and test actual app compatibility/performance. The product already packages `vulkan.pastel` as a software graphics path (`a6l-software-graphics.mk`), so “no Vulkan anywhere” also needs qualification. Record the renderer actually selected on the installed phone. Do not advertise unsupported hardware Vulkan capability. [Mesa's driver documentation](https://docs.mesa3d.org/drivers/freedreno.html).

2. **H5, H8, S2, S6, H40 and headlines — reconcile current work.** Per Pierre, Claude owns V75 integration and the light-sensor fix; “nobody owns it” is obsolete. `dualux-20260925.md` documents the stock frontlight wiring, the `leds-pwm` replacement overlay and an implemented/offline-tested switcher. Frontlight physical operation remains unproven, but its existence no longer needs to be rediscovered. Use the newer dualux key behavior instead of H8's older short-press mirror rule. Add dualux, its app, frontlight overlay/module and composer patch to S2's explicit integration inventory.

3. **S21/headline 9 — the work has been committed.** HEAD is `9e7af7b`, dated 25 September; initial status showed only the active `kernel/stk3338/` directory untracked. Remove the claim that 153 changes remain uncommitted since 21 September. A local commit does not establish an off-machine backup; track that separately if needed.

4. **S18 and `rom/rom.mk` — correct the RAM inventory.** `captures/stock-readonly-20260920/cpuinfo.txt:13` reports `MemTotal: 6291456 kB`, and `hardware-readiness-20260920.md` identifies 6 GB. `rom.mk:37` calls it 4 GiB and selects the 4096 heap profile. A conservative heap profile can be intentional; it must not be justified by the wrong hardware capacity. Recheck usable memory/reservations in V75 before tuning zram, ART and lmkd.

5. **S13 — match add-ons to the actual platform.** This tree's build checkpoint records Android 17/API 37; the checklist proposes MindTheGapps for Android 16. Replace the version-specific recommendation with a compatibility check against the actual built system's SDK, ABI and supported add-on release. Availability of a suitable package was not verified in this review. Do not promise any Play Integrity tier from configuration alone.

6. **H59 — encryption needs kernel and key-management work, not just a fstab flag.** The saved V67 config has `CONFIG_FS_ENCRYPTION` disabled (line 7123); the ROM fstab explicitly uses plain ext4. Add kernel support, filesystem/key lifecycle and recovery validation to this item. FBE and metadata encryption are separate tasks. AOSP recommends a non-inline configuration when inline hardware is not used; review the proposed `inlinecrypt_optimized` choice instead of copying it mechanically. Test credential-encrypted data before first unlock, PIN changes, reboot, reset and update. Software KeyMint must not be described as hardware-backed protection. [AOSP FBE implementation guidance](https://source.android.com/docs/security/features/encryption/file-based).

7. **H25 — make the CSFB limitation time-bound.** Orange says its remaining metropolitan 2G shutdown runs from 22 September to 20 October 2026, and 3G closure is planned for 2028. A successful call today is not a long-term telephony solution. Record the actual voice RAT during calls and verify 3G fallback in the intended coverage area. Keep IMS feasibility as an explicit unresolved decision; a generic SIP client is not proof of carrier IMS integration. [Orange 2G schedule](https://reseaux.orange.fr/actualites/arret-de-la-2g-orange-etes-vous-concerne.html), [Orange 2G/3G plan](https://reseaux.orange.fr/nos-reseaux/modernisation-des-reseaux/arret-2g-3g).

8. **Status/estimates — separate evidence levels.** H26 combines “built” with a phone reset; H38 calls motion sensors proven when only IIO enumeration is established. Track `built`, `packaged`, `kernel tested`, `Android app tested`, `suspend/reboot tested` independently. Include the exact kernel/DT/modules/vendor/system hashes and test date. Replace the confident overall 6–10 week estimate with milestone estimates after hardware results; IPA, camera, power management and IMS are research uncertainties.

## Telephony entries that need finer acceptance criteria

- **Cell broadcast deserves its own row**, including emergency alerts and user settings. It is currently buried in optional H24. `RadioMessagingVoice.cpp:122` accepts the channel configuration without programming it; activation also reports success on a best-effort basis. Do not treat a successful HAL response as working alerts. Review with recorded/offline vectors or an appropriate authorized test facility.
- **SMS needs more than one CLI message:** multipart and Unicode SMS, delivery reports, messages arriving asleep, reconnect/redelivery, and framework acknowledgement. MMS requires its own APN/data-context validation.
- **SIM Toolkit/UICC functions need an explicit support decision:** STK proactive commands, SIM contacts if wanted, PIN/PUK flows and card-state changes. Test lock/error handling with mocks or a dedicated test SIM, not by exhausting retries on Pierre's SIM.
- **Data attach needs a real acceptance test:** `RadioNetworkData.cpp:386` accepts `setInitialAttachApn` without programming it. Record whether the intended carrier needs this and test fresh attach rather than relying on preserved stock modem state.
- **Emergency-call review is necessary but not proof of service.** Preserve H23's prohibition on casual live tests. Review no-SIM/locked-SIM/out-of-service routing, error paths and audio; record unvalidated behavior plainly. Any end-to-end testing needs an appropriate coordinated facility/operator process.

## Hardware already covered or intentionally excluded

LCD/GPU, both touch panels, e-ink bridge/PMIC, keys, haptics, speaker/headset/microphones, modem, Wi-Fi/BT/GNSS, three cameras plus focus actuator and flash, ALS/proximity, motion sensors, hall, USB, charger/fuel gauge/thermal, storage, RTC and watchdog all have existing rows. They do not need duplicate driver tasks.

Keep fingerprint explicitly deferred per Pierre. Keep NFC listed as not fitted unless new board evidence contradicts it. microSD and notification LED remain applicability checks; neither a generic DT node nor `nosdcard` alone settles physical presence. No new evidence here establishes IR, barometer, wireless charging or USB video output, so do not invent required ports for them.

The personal daily-driver milestone is deliberately less demanding than official LineageOS support. The official requirements are useful as a cross-check, but include stock-feature parity and release obligations beyond this milestone: for example front/rear camera/video, hardware codecs, encryption, fingerprint where fitted and native updater/recovery. A successful personal build with documented omissions should not be called official-support-ready. [LineageOS device support requirements](https://github.com/LineageOS/charter/blob/main/device-support-requirements.md).

## Suggested V75 review gate

1. Publish one manifest of the exact combined kernel, DT, modules, firmware, HALs, properties and system/vendor images. Include all late fixes and dualux. Check DT resource conflicts, firmware paths, module dependencies, load ordering and duplicate init/HAL ownership.
2. Finish offline image/bootloader, VINTF and policy checks; record optional drivers that remain disabled or experimental. Do not infer device success from QEMU module loads.
3. In an attended installed-system run, prove rollback access, persistent storage, display/input, USB, battery/charging and thermal behavior. Then validate through Android apps: calls/SMS, Wi-Fi, sound, sensors, e-ink and camera as each becomes available.
4. Repeat functional checks after cold boot and suspend, with charger disconnected, and across face switches and concurrent uses. Preserve dated logs and expected-versus-observed results; success markers alone cannot establish audibility, image quality or battery life.
5. Before calling it a daily driver, resolve or explicitly accept the remaining omissions: single SIM, no IMS, security limitations, nonworking sensors/cameras/accessories, and update/recovery constraints. Keep research estimates separate from completed acceptance tests.
