## Changes and reasons

Camera HAL advertises processed sizes derived from actual A6L RAW10 modes;
Aperture prefers the corresponding supported size for ordinary 4:3 JPEG
photography. This avoids the expensive RAW resize used to preserve full field
of view at generic output sizes. Video, high-resolution and RAW preferences
remain unchanged. On r6r, a bounded wide-camera probe reduced ISP median from
192.649 to 51.789 ms and warm callback median from 196 to 55 ms, approximately
5.1 to 18.2 updates/s. Both sessions saved and closed. This demonstrates the
native route, not normal-app selection, stock photo quality or sustained video
performance. Main-camera speed still needs measurement at its larger native size.

The serial e-ink mirror reads complete KMS plane tuples and rejects captures
when the display configuration changes during copying. Geometry query failures
cannot silently invent defaults. This reduces one source of mixed captures;
it does not lock producer buffers against future reuse. Frequent animation may
cause repeated rejection, so static and animated acceptance need phone checks.

The daemon adds selectable ordered dithering. The existing error-diffusion
conversion can propagate a one-pixel change into almost half a flat raster in
a reproduced fixture. Ordered conversion keeps that change local, at the cost
of a visible fixed grid or banding in some images. Clean-install default remains
error diffusion. This phone's next-boot trial selects ordered using the existing
`persist.vendor.eink.dither` property; only service startup reads it. Electrical
waveforms, panel clocks, rails and voltage sequences retain r6r's values.

r6r's Auto bookkeeping and selected-mode settle are retained. Its 100-second
static Settings trial emitted one forced 79-frame quality cleanup and three
ordinary 39-frame quality updates. The latter may still be visually intrusive;
the result does not establish that all pages are quiet or that flashing is gone.

## Verification and limits

Independent camera, dither and startup-selector reviews passed. Native camera
fixtures exercise real debayer geometry and Kotlin selection; e-ink fixtures
exercise real capture/composition and conversion. The final image audit permits
only Aperture APK/compiled files, camera HAL, mirror, daemon, its init arguments
and build metadata. IPA signature and APK signing certificate verify; all other
files, including kernel, graphics driver, policy and tuning, are retained.

The experimental concurrent-capture prototype is excluded. Optical moving
artifacts, repeated visible sharpening, photo calibration, video quality and
audio recording remain open until measured. Evidence and current state are in
`firmware/extracted/rom-r6s-20261003`.
